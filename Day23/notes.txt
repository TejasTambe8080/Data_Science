🧠 PART 1 — CENTRAL TENDENCY

Central tendency means:

👉 "What is the center of the data?"

1. Mean (Average)

Formula:

Example:

data = [10,20,30,40,50]

Mean:

30
Python
import numpy as np

np.mean(data)
Industry Usage

Used for:

Average salary
Average sales
Average customer spending
Problem With Mean

Dataset:

[10,20,30,40,1000]

Mean becomes:

220

Not representative.

Outliers affect mean heavily.

2. Median

Middle value after sorting.

Dataset:

[10,20,30,40,1000]

Median:

30
Python
np.median(data)
Industry Usage

Used in:

Income analysis
House prices
Salary reports

Because it is resistant to outliers.

3. Mode

Most frequent value.

Dataset:

[10,20,20,30,40]

Mode:

20
Python
from scipy import stats

stats.mode(data)
Industry Usage
Most sold product
Most common category
Customer preferences
📊 PART 2 — PERCENTILES

Percentiles tell us:

"What percentage of data lies below this point?"

Example

90th percentile:

Means:

90% of observations are below it.

10% are above it.

Python
np.percentile(data,90)
Industry Usage
Top customers
Top performers
Salary benchmarking

📈 PART 4 — DISTRIBUTION BASICS
What is Distribution?

Distribution shows:

How data is spread.

Example:

Employee salaries:

[30000,35000,40000,45000,50000]

Tightly grouped.

Another:

[30000,40000,50000,100000,500000]

Highly spread.

Visualize:

import seaborn as sns

sns.histplot(data,kde=True)
