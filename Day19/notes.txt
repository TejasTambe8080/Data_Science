🧠 PART 1 — THEORY (INTERVIEW LEVEL)
🔹 What is Correlation?

👉 Measures relationship between two variables

🔹 Correlation Values
Value	Meaning
+1	Strong positive
0	No relation
-1	Strong negative
🔹 Example:
Salary ↑ → Experience ↑ → positive correlation
Price ↑ → Demand ↓ → negative correlation
⚠️ IMPORTANT CONCEPT
🔹 Correlation ≠ Causation

👉 Just because two variables are related
❌ doesn’t mean one causes the other

💻 PART 2 — CALCULATE CORRELATION

🔹 Specific Column
